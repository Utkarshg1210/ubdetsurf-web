About UBDetsurf: chemistry in service of agriculture

Our story

Built on research, innovation and trust

UBDetsurf Industries Pvt. Ltd. has been developing specialty chemical solutions for agrochemical formulations since 2009, focusing on emulsifiers, surfactants, wetting / dispersing agents and adjuvants.

Our approach is grounded in research and innovation, customer-centric thinking and QCS — Quality Consistency, Cost Effectiveness and Pre- & Post-Sale Services.

Headquartered in Noida, UBDetsurf has expanded its footprint across India through customer relationships, strategic partnerships and dedicated sales and support.

See the products we make, how our R&D team works, and our ISO certifications.

Leadership

Words from our directors

The people guiding UBDetsurf’s research, relationships and growth.

Marketing & Operations

Umesh Gupta

“Great marketing creates demand; great operations earns trust. Lasting success comes when both work as one.”

With over 25 years in the agro-industry, Umesh Gupta began as a Sales Officer in 1993, founded a trading enterprise in 1995 dealing in surfactants, specialty chemicals and packaging materials, and turned it into a manufacturing business with a dedicated production facility in 2009.

As Director – Marketing & Operations, he shapes UBDetsurf’s market presence and operational capabilities, combining customer-centric marketing, product understanding and manufacturing discipline. For him, lasting relationships rest on understanding customer needs, consistent quality and honouring every commitment.

Umesh Gupta, Director – Marketing & Operations

Technology & Business Development

B.K. Gupta

“Technology creates products, but trust creates businesses.”

With over four decades across specialty chemicals, agrochemicals, surfactants, emulsifiers and strategic business management — including his role as Chief Operating Officer of the RR Group — B.K. Gupta has built a career on turning scientific expertise into commercially viable solutions.

Since 2009, he has spearheaded technology development and product innovation at UBDetsurf across surfactants, emulsifiers, wetting and dispersing agents and adjuvants, helping manufacturers deliver safer, more effective and environmentally responsible crop protection. His philosophy is simple: listen before advising, solve before selling, and build partnerships rather than transactions.

B.K. Gupta, Director – Technology & Business Development
