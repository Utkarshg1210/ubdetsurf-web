Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

What does UBDetsurf Industries manufacture?

UBDetsurf Industries Pvt. Ltd. manufactures emulsifiers, surfactants, wetting and dispersing agents and adjuvants for agrochemical (crop-protection) formulations. Its recommendation tables cover EC, EW, SC, SL, WP/WDG, SG and granule formulations, and the team also supports microemulsions, adjuvants and tank-mix additives. See the full product range.

Who does UBDetsurf supply?

UBDetsurf supplies surfactants and formulation support to agrochemical manufacturers and formulators across India. A selection of customers is listed on the Our Clients page.

Does UBDetsurf support custom formulation requirements?

Yes. The R&D team develops surfactants, emulsifiers, adjuvants, dispersing agents and wetting agents to match customer requirements and works with customers from concept to commercial scale. Read more on the Research & Development page.

How can I request product specifications or dosage guidance?

Email infosales@ubdetsurf.com or call 0120-4136200, and mention the formulation type, active ingredient and target market. Recommended dosages and ratios depend on the formulation and the technical material used, so they are shared on request. You can also use the enquiry form.

Where is UBDetsurf located?

The corporate and head office is at Ithum Tower-B, Sector-62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201301, India. The company operates two manufacturing units, with a third planned.

What are surfactants and adjuvants used for in agrochemical formulations?

In general, surfactants (surface-active agents) help an active ingredient mix with water, stay dispersed and wet or spread on a leaf surface. Emulsifiers are typically used in EC and EW formulations, wetting and dispersing agents in suspension and powder formulations, and adjuvants or tank-mix additives are added to improve spreading, sticking and penetration of a spray. This is general technical background; the right product depends on the formulation, so ask the UBDetsurf team for a recommendation.
