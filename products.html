Product questions

Frequently asked questions

Which formulation types does UBDetsurf cover?

The recommendation tables on this page cover EC (emulsifiable concentrate), EW (emulsion in water), SC (suspension concentrate), SL (soluble liquid), WP / WG / WDG / SP / SG / WS (wettable powders, granules and soluble powders) and GR / SG (granules and water-soluble granules). UBDetsurf also supports adjuvants and tank-mix additives, microemulsions (ME) and custom formulations on enquiry.

What do the tables on this page show?

Each row pairs a UBDetsurf product with the active ingredients (molecules) it is recommended for in that formulation type.

Are the recommendations a guarantee of performance?

No. They are based on ideal laboratory conditions, and actual products and dosages may vary with the formulation and the technical material used. Ratios may also vary with the technical and the solvents used. Please contact UBDetsurf for detailed dosages and formulation support.

How do I get dosage and formulation support?

Email infosales@ubdetsurf.com, call 0120-4136200, or use the enquiry form and describe the active ingredient, formulation type and target market.
