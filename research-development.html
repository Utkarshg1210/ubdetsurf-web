Development process

From concept to commercial scale

  1. Step 1

    Client brief & requirements analysis

    We begin by understanding your active ingredient, target crop, application method, regulatory market and performance requirements.

  2. Step 2

    Formulation screening & design

    Our scientists design and screen candidate formulations, evaluating surfactant systems, co-solvents and stabilisers for compatibility and performance.

  3. Step 3

    Laboratory testing & optimisation

    Short-listed surfactant systems undergo studies with pesticide formulations, including performance, stability and physicochemical studies.

  4. Step 4

    Pilot scale-up & validation

    The optimised formulation is manufactured at pilot scale to validate process parameters and confirm quality specifications.

  5. Step 5

    Commercial manufacturing & supply

    After successful validation, production transfers to commercial scale with QA protocols, ready for market launch and ongoing supply.

R&D questions

Frequently asked questions

What does UBDetsurf's R&D team do?

The team develops surfactants, emulsifiers, adjuvants, dispersing agents and wetting agents to match customer requirements, and helps pesticide manufacturers with formulation development, shelf life and formulation efficiency.

What can the R&D facility test?

The facility is described as equipped for formulation preparation, physical and chemical characterisation, particle-size analysis, wet milling of solid pesticides and stability testing at different temperatures. It also lists granular, WDG and encapsulation facilities.

How does a development project start?

It starts with a brief covering the active ingredient, target crop, application method, regulatory market and performance requirements. Send it through the enquiry form or email infosales@ubdetsurf.com.
